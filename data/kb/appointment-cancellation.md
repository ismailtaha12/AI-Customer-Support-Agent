# Appointment cancellation and rescheduling

## Cancelling an appointment
You can cancel or reschedule any appointment free of charge up to 24 hours before the start time. Use the "My appointments" page in the Nile Health app, or reply to your reminder email.

## Late cancellations and no-shows
Cancellations made less than 24 hours before the appointment are charged a late fee of 150 EGP. If you miss an appointment without cancelling (a no-show), the full consultation fee is charged.

## Exceptions
The late fee is waived for medical emergencies and for technical failures on our side, such as a doctor being unable to join a video visit. Contact support within 48 hours and attach any proof, such as a hospital note.

## Rescheduling limits
Each appointment can be rescheduled twice. After that it must be cancelled and booked again.
