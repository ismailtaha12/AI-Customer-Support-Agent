# Billing, payments and refunds

## Accepted payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard and Meeza cards, as well as Fawry and mobile wallet payments. Payment is taken when you book.

## Refunds
If you cancel at least 24 hours before your appointment, you receive a full refund. Refunds go back to the original payment method and take 5 to 10 business days to appear, depending on your bank.

## Duplicate or wrong charges
If you were charged twice for the same appointment, open a support ticket with the date and last four digits of the card. Confirmed duplicate charges are refunded in full within 7 business days.

## Invoices
Invoices are emailed after every completed visit and can be downloaded from the "Billing" page. We cannot issue invoices in a company name after the fact, so enter company details when booking.
