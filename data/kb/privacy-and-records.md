# Privacy and medical records

## Who can see your records
Only you and the clinicians treating you can see your medical records. Support agents never see clinical notes. They can only see appointment times and billing status.

## Requesting a copy of your records
You can request a copy of your records from Settings > Privacy > Request my data. Requests are fulfilled within 14 days and delivered as a password-protected PDF.

## Deleting your account
You can delete your account at any time from Settings. Medical records must be kept for a legal minimum of 10 years, so they are archived and made inaccessible rather than deleted. Non-clinical data such as marketing preferences is deleted within 30 days.

## Sharing data with third parties
We never sell personal data. Records are shared with other providers only when you give explicit consent in the app.
