# Video visit troubleshooting

## Before your visit
Join from the app 5 minutes early. Allow camera and microphone access when asked. A connection of at least 2 Mbps is recommended.

## No video or no sound
First check that the browser or app has camera and microphone permission in your device settings. Close other apps that use the camera, such as Zoom or Teams. If it still fails, leave and rejoin the visit.

## Poor connection
Switch from mobile data to Wi-Fi, or move closer to the router. Turning off your own camera improves audio quality while the doctor's video stays on.

## If the doctor cannot join
If the doctor has not joined within 10 minutes of the start time, tap "Report a problem" in the visit screen. You will be rescheduled for free and any late fees are waived.
